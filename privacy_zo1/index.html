Privacy Policy

Platform: Z01 / Bidly • Parent: Zuntra • Governing Law: DPDP Act, 2023 & IT Act, 2000 (India) • Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how [Bidly Legal Entity Name], a subsidiary of Zuntra ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal data of users of the Bidly and Z01 marketplace platform. It is published in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable rules, including the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

The platform is an online marketplace where users can rent studios, book gear and equipment, hire creative talent, and place bids. This Policy covers our web applications, mobile applications, and services.

1. Definitions

  • 1.1 "Personal Data": Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, as defined under the DPDP Act.
  • 1.2 "Data Principal": You, the individual using the Platform, whether as a Bidder/Renter, a Vendor/Seller, or a visitor.
  • 1.3 "Data Fiduciary": The Company, which determines the purpose and means of processing your Personal Data.
  • 1.4 "Bidder / Renter": A User who places a bid or books a listing on the Platform.
  • 1.5 "Seller / Vendor": A User who creates a Listing (studio, equipment, manpower, or service) and invites bids/bookings.
  • 1.6 "Listing": An item, studio, gear, service, or creative talent profile posted on the Platform on which bids or bookings can be placed.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to Personal Data collected through the Platform from Bidders, Renters, Sellers, Vendors, and visitors to our mobile applications and websites.

4. Sensitive Data: Verification and Financial Information

  • 4.1 Identity Documents: Verification and payout documents are collected strictly to verify identity, satisfy KYC mandates, prevent fraud, and disburse payouts. Documents are shared only as detailed in Clause 7.
  • 4.2 Payment Credentials: Payment card, UPI, and net-banking transactions are processed through RBI-authorized payment gateways. We do not store full card numbers or sensitive authentication PINs.
  • 4.3 Contact Sharing: Your delivery address and direct contact details are shared only with the verified party involved in a completed booking or transaction with you.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and maintain your account and verify your identity through KYC.
  • Enable Vendors to post listings and Users to place bids, book studios, rent equipment, and hire talent.
  • Process payments, security deposits, refunds, and payouts, and issue invoices.
  • Facilitate delivery, studio check-in, and service fulfilment between counterparties.
  • Send service updates, push notifications via OneSignal, bid alerts, and marketing communications (where opted in).
  • Detect and prevent fraud, shill bidding, bid manipulation, and fake accounts.
  • Resolve disputes, handle support inquiries, and comply with applicable statutory laws.

6. Consent

  • 6.1 By registering and using the Platform, you consent to the collection and processing described in this Policy. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer, subject to limitations on certain service capabilities.
  • 6.2 Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing already concluded, and we retain records required by law (e.g., tax, GST, and audit logs).

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your Personal Data. We share it only in the following defined circumstances:

  • Transaction Counterparts: To the extent necessary to complete transactions (e.g., vendor sees booking contact; user sees vendor public studio details).
  • Service Providers: Secure payment gateways (Razorpay), cloud infrastructure, SMS / OTP providers, and push notification services (OneSignal).
  • Public Community Data: Your public username, ratings, and reviews left on listings.
  • Legal & Regulatory Demands: With law enforcement, courts, or regulators when mandated under Indian law.
  • Corporate Restructuring: In connection with any merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, subject to equivalent privacy obligations.

8. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data for the duration necessary to deliver the services or comply with statutory requirements. Upon account deletion requests, personal data is purged or anonymized, except where transaction and tax records must be retained under applicable statutory periods.

9. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption at rest, access management, and vulnerability assessments per the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
