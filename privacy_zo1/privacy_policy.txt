1. Definitions

1.1 "Personal Data" any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, as defined under the DPDP Act.

1.2 "Data Principal" you, the individual using the Platform, whether as a Bidder, a Seller or a visitor.

1.3 "Data Fiduciary" the Company, which determines the purpose and means of processing your Personal Data.

1.4 "Bidder" a User who places a bid on a Listing.

1.5 "Seller" a User who creates a Listing and invites bids.

1.6 "Listing" an item, service or project posted on the Platform on which bids can be placed.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to Personal Data collected through the Bidly Platform from Bidders, Sellers and visitors to the Bidly website.

5. How We Use Your Information

5.1 We use your information to:

a. Create and maintain your account and verify your identity

b. Enable Sellers to create Listings and Bidders to place bids, and to determine and communicate winning bids

c. Process payments, refunds and payouts, and issue invoices

d. Facilitate delivery or fulfilment between the Bidder and the Seller

e. Send service-related communications, bid alerts and, where you opt in, marketing communications

f. Detect and prevent fraud, including shill bidding, bid manipulation and fake accounts

g. Resolve disputes and provide customer support

h. Comply with applicable law

6. Consent

6.1 By registering, you consent to the processing described in this Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer (Clause 12), which may limit your ability to use certain features.

6.2 Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing already done, and we may continue to keep data we are required to retain by law, for example transaction records.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

7.1 We do not sell your Personal Data. We share it only:

a. With the other party to a transaction, to the extent necessary to complete it. For example, a Seller sees the delivery details of the winning Bidder, and a Bidder sees the Seller's public profile and ratings

b. With payment gateway, identity verification, logistics, cloud hosting and communication providers who support Platform operations

c. With other users, only your username, public profile, ratings and, where a Listing shows bids, your bid amounts under your username or a masked ID. [Confirm bid visibility.]

d. With regulators, law enforcement or courts where required by law

e. In connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to equivalent protection

f. With your explicit consent, for any other purpose
