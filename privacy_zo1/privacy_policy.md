# Privacy Policy — Bidly / Z01

*Last Updated: October 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how **[Bidly Legal Entity Name]**, a subsidiary of **Zuntra** ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal data of users of the platform. It is published in accordance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act")**, the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, and applicable rules, including the **Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021**.

The platform is an online marketplace where users can place bids, rent studios, equipment, and hire talent. This Policy covers our web applications, mobile applications, and websites.

---

## 1. Definitions

- **1.1 "Personal Data":** Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, as defined under the DPDP Act.
- **1.2 "Data Principal":** You, the individual using the Platform, whether as a Bidder/Renter, a Vendor/Seller, or a visitor.
- **1.3 "Data Fiduciary":** The Company, which determines the purpose and means of processing your Personal Data.
- **1.4 "Bidder / Renter":** A User who places a bid or books a listing on the Platform.
- **1.5 "Seller / Vendor":** A User who creates a listing (studios, equipment, talent, services) and invites bookings/bids.
- **1.6 "Listing":** An item, studio, gear, service, or talent posted on the Platform.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to Personal Data collected through our Platform from Bidders, Renters, Sellers, Vendors, and visitors.

## 4. Sensitive Data: Verification and Financial Information

- **4.1** Identity documents and payout details are collected only to verify you, prevent fraud, and process payments/payouts, and are shared only as described in Clause 7.
- **4.2** Payment card, UPI, and bank details are handled securely by our certified payment gateway. We do not store full card numbers or sensitive payment authorization credentials.
- **4.3** Your contact/delivery address is shared only with the other party involved in a completed booking or transaction with you.

---

## 5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
1. Create and maintain your account and verify your identity (KYC).
2. Enable Vendors to create Listings and Users to book studios, rent gear, hire talent, and place bids.
3. Process payments, refunds, and payouts, and issue invoices.
4. Facilitate service fulfilment, equipment delivery, and booking coordination.
5. Send push notifications, OTPs, booking updates, and service alerts.
6. Detect and prevent fraud, account takeovers, and fraudulent listings.
7. Resolve disputes and provide customer support.
8. Comply with applicable legal, tax, and statutory obligations.

## 6. Consent

- **6.1** By registering and using the Platform, you consent to the processing described in this Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer, which may limit your ability to use certain features.
- **6.2** Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing already completed, and we may retain data required by law (e.g., financial and transaction records).

## 7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your Personal Data. We share it only:
- **With Transaction Counterparties:** Necessary contact and booking details are shared between renters and vendors to fulfil bookings.
- **With Service Providers:** Trusted cloud hosting, SMS/notification gateways (OneSignal), identity verification, and payment gateways (Razorpay).
- **Public Profile:** Your public display name, avatar, and ratings/reviews left on listings.
- **Legal & Regulatory Authorities:** When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental demand.
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with any merger, sale of assets, or corporate restructuring, subject to equivalent data protection standards.

We retain Personal Data as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, or as required by law. Upon account deletion requests, personal data is erased or anonymized, except where retention is legally mandated for tax, accounting, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention.

## 10. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a summary of your personal data processed by us.
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or outdated personal data.
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data no longer needed for its original purpose.
- **Withdraw Consent:** Revoke previously granted consent for data processing.
- **Grievance Redressal:** Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer.
- **Nomination:** Nominate a person to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.
